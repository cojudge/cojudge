Collect anagrams from a string array.

You receive an array `strs` holding strings. Partition its entries into collections so that words placed in the same collection are rearrangements of one another — that is, they share exactly the same multiset of letters. The sequence of the collections in your answer carries no importance.

**Constraints:**

- `1 ≤ strs.length ≤ 10^4`
- `0 ≤ strs[i].length ≤ 100`
- Each `strs[i]` is composed solely of lowercase English letters.
